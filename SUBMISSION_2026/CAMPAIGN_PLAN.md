## Headline

Run the decomposition on IRRIGATION, not flood. Pre-registration v1 §3.1 is arithmetically self-falsifying — the maximum attainable primary contrast in flood is 0.85 pp against a pre-committed ±5 pp equivalence margin, so §12 returns "claim falsified" with probability ≈1 for reasons unrelated to truth. Both attack lanes derived this independently and the run matrix reached it structurally; I take all three over the pre-registration. The committed campaign is 333 GPU-hours / ~112 runs in five blocks: a 12 h instrument gate, a 32 h clean-tree bridge to the published pair, a six-arm ladder on irrigation gemma3:4b at 5 seeds (149 h), a five-arm replication on ministral3:3b at 5 seeds (112 h), and a fully-identified flood regime control restructured to 1,000 agents × 1 year (28 h). That is the advisor's size, not the run matrix's 556 h — the binding constraint is eight pages and one student's attention, not the GPU, and 149 runs is 149 integrity reviews during the writing window. The scale axis (ministral3:8b, gemma3:12b, 170 h) is cut on the advisor's own argument: the pre-registration already commits to labelling the scale interaction exploratory regardless of p-value, so 24 runs would buy a term we have pre-committed not to claim. Eight zero-GPU code fixes land before the tag; the placebo arm currently renders the blocked skill name and the real suggested_correction, which alone would have published a false negative on G_content. Primary outcome moves from V_world to V_intent (attempt-0 antecedent, last agent-proposed consequent, defined on 100 % of rows, free of the registry-default artifact); year 1 is the unconditional primary window; the equivalence margin becomes scale-relative. The committed line finishes 25 August, three weeks before the 15 September freeze — the workshop paper is written into that slack, and Nature Water v29 goes to Yang on 8 August, before the queue needs any attention at all.

## Pre-registration summary

PRIMARY TESTBED: irrigation (78 agents x 42 yr, 3,276 decisions/run), where enforced rules fire on 28.1-62.6 % of decisions. Flood (1,000 agents x 1 yr) is retained as a pre-registered FLATNESS PREDICTION, never as a falsification test - F1 and F4 may only be evaluated in a cell where V(A) >= 10 pp, a fire-rate floor flood fails by construction and irrigation clears by 3-6x. This reverses pre-registration v1 §3.1 and is recorded in a dated SUPERSESSION.md committed before any data exists; v1 remains in git history unedited. PRIMARY OUTCOME: V_intent - the theory-violation rate on enforced set E with the rule antecedent read from the ATTEMPT-0 self-reported appraisal (pre-treatment: the base prompt is byte-identical across arms, so attempt 0 is drawn before any rule text exists in any arm) and the consequent read from the LAST AGENT-PROPOSED skill in attempt_skills. V_intent is premise-invariant by construction (an agent that restates its appraisal so the rule no longer applies still counts as violating) and is the only one of the three candidate outcomes defined on 100 % of rows AND free of the registry-default artifact. V_world (executed action) and V_choice (restricted to non-fallback rows, reported as bounds only) appear beside it in every table and are never hidden. This takes the methodologist over pre-registration v1, which made V_world primary. PRIMARY CONTRAST: V_intent(A) - V_intent(C1), paired on seed, n=5, YEAR-1 WINDOW UNCONDITIONALLY PRIMARY (v1's 'year 1 leads only if the windows disagree in sign' is an outcome-contingent choice of primary window and is closed). DECOMPOSITION IDENTITY 1 (ladder, telescoping, EXACT BY CONSTRUCTION and presented as a single-order path-dependent attribution, never as evidence): V(A)-V(C1) = G_sample[A->A'] + G_wrapper[A'->A''] + G_pressure[A''->B'] + G_content[B'->B] + G_enforce[B->C1]. Order-sensitivity guard: if |G_wrapper| >= 2 pp the terms are reported as order-dependent with the order named in the caption; the ladder is never re-ordered after seeing which order tells a cleaner story. IDENTITY 2 (composition): V_final(A)-V_final(C1) = [V(A)-V(C1)] + Gamma(C1), with Gamma(A)=0 exactly, and Gamma further split into Gamma_within (antecedent movement inside retained rows) and Gamma_set (rows entering or leaving the at-risk set) - reporting Gamma as one number would reproduce, in this paper's own headline statistic, the exact conflation the paper indicts. IDENTITY 3 (mechanism partition, must sum to 1.0000): FILTER + ACTION_REV + PREMISE_REV_PURE + JOINT + PERSIST; failure to sum is a bug, halts analysis, is fixed and logged, and is never reported as a finding. PREMISE REVISION GETS A NOISE FLOOR: M+ = P(appraisal moves out of the rule antecedent band between attempt 0 and final | at-risk and attempt-0 violated), reported in every arm, with free drift = M+(A'), content-attributable = M+(B)-M+(B'), enforcement-attributable = M+(C1)-M+(B). Raw M+(C1) is reported but never as the headline: on a 4B model at default temperature spontaneous ordinal-label instability between two draws could plausibly be 20-40 %, and a headline of 'premise revision accounted for X pp' against a free-resample floor of 0.6X would be wrong by that factor. EQUIVALENCE MARGIN: scale-relative, max(1 pp, 0.25 x V(A) in that cell), computed on the control arm whose value is arm-independent at year 1, with the predicted margin recorded from the Gate-0 V-proxy before the campaign so the number is on record ahead of data. A fixed +/-5 pp applied to both a 0.85 pp regime and a 45 pp regime is not one decision rule wearing one number, it is two. FALSIFIERS, each reported as falsification in the abstract if it fires, in the position the headline would otherwise occupy: F1 the composite is negligible (Gamma(C1) within margin AND p_FILTER <= 5 % AND p_ACTION_REV >= 80 % of classified gain, in both configurations); F2 the instrument is void (attempt-0 balance fails in the anchor, or the byte-identity prompt assertion fails anywhere) - the paper is not submitted on that data; F3 the content term is not identified (B/B' call matching or placebo byte-identity fails) - G_content is reported as 'not identified', and identity 2 survives because it does not depend on the ladder; F4 the effect is resampling (G_sample largest while G_content and G_enforce are both within margin) - what the field calls governance is a second draw from the same distribution, and that is more useful than the claim would have been; F5 mechanism ordering reverses (p_ACTION_REV > p_PREMISE_REV_PURE + p_JOINT) - every sentence implying otherwise is removed; F6 (NEW) premise revision is noise (M+(B)-M+(B') and M+(C1)-M+(B) both within margin while raw M+(C1) is large). HELD-OUT RULE, at zero marginal GPU: irrigation ships high_threat_no_maintain at level WARNING, and a WARNING has no channel to the model in this implementation, so it is inert by construction, the agent cannot know it exists, and it is scored in every arm and enforced in none inside runs already being done. Reported as the CONDITIONAL rate (violation given the action class the rule constrains) alongside the marginal, because the marginal falls for free whenever enforcement shifts action composition. Consequent disjointness from the enforced set must be verified before the tag. STATISTICAL MODEL: unit of analysis is the SEED for a per-cell claim (paired t at df=4, Wilcoxon robustness, and the n=5 exact sign-flip permutation floor of 0.0625 stated in the main text, not a footnote - no 5-seed cell can reach p<0.05 by permutation and the paper says so). The decision-level GLMM clusters at the RUN, not the agent: the flood runner writes a population aggregate into every agent's memory each year, so agents are not conditionally independent and effective n for any post-year-1 claim is 5, not 5,000. Absence is established by TOST, never by non-significance. The scale interaction is not tested - both scale models are cut. AMENDMENT POLICY UNCHANGED: no outcome, threshold, exclusion, or interpretation may be redefined, re-scoped, softened, or demoted after any data exists; the G1 gate's downscopes are legitimate because G1 runs and is written up BEFORE the pre-registration is committed, under a separate earlier tag.
