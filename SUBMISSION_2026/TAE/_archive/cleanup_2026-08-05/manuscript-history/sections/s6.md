## 6 What it does to the statistic <!-- page budget: 1.0 pg -->

<!-- DRAFT NOTE, clear before the number freeze: this section uses 18 {{CLAIM:...}} markers, 15 of which substitute.py does not define yet. Names, values and NUMBERS.md sources are listed in the drafting report; `python substitute.py --check` will fail until they are added. -->

The statistic a governance layer publishes is conditional on a label the agent wrote. Removing that
condition gives a different number over a denominator the design fixes in advance, 78 agents by 42
years, {{CLAIM:decisions_per_run}} decisions per run in both arms, none of it authored by the model.
Figure 3 carries both numbers for the five irrigation cells.

On the anchor cell the effect survives de-conditioning almost intact,
{{CLAIM:anchor_cond_delta_bare}} conditional against {{CLAIM:anchor_uncond_delta_bare}}
unconditional, a gap of {{CLAIM:anchor_gap}}. The three other balanced cells widen from there.
gemma4:e2b goes from {{CLAIM:e2b_cond_delta}} to {{CLAIM:e2b_uncond_delta}}, gemma4:e4b from
{{CLAIM:e4b_cond_delta}} to {{CLAIM:e4b_uncond_delta}}, and gemma3:12b from
{{CLAIM:b12_cond_delta_bare}} to {{CLAIM:b12_uncond_delta_bare}}.

On gemma3:12b essentially the whole apparent effect is composition, {{CLAIM:b12_cond_delta}} becoming
{{CLAIM:b12_uncond_delta}}. The conditioning variable's distribution moved and the increase rate over
a fixed denominator did not, so a reader given only the conditional number would credit the
governance layer with a behaviour change this corpus does not show.

On ministral3:8b the two statistics disagree in sign: conditional {{CLAIM:ministral_cond_delta}}
against unconditional {{CLAIM:ministral_uncond_delta}}, on three paired seeds at df = 2.

The unconditional rate is not a corrected version of the conditional one. It answers how often the
agent asked for more water out of every decision it made, where the conditional rate answers how
often it asked on the decisions where it reported high scarcity. What survives de-conditioning on the
anchor is a quantity of similar size, not the same quantity.

Both rates use the LAST-PROPOSED action reading, the one the published statistics use. Substituting
the executed action on rejected rows moves the gemma3:12b unconditional delta to
{{CLAIM:b12_uncond_exec_delta}}, so the near-zero result on that cell belongs to the published reading
and has to be reported as belonging to it.

A denominator fixed by design also admits an external reference the conditional rate cannot use. The
irrigation action set holds five skills, two of them increases, so a uniformly random agent would
propose an increase on 40% of its decisions. Without validators the unconditional rate
sits far above that null ({{CLAIM:anchor_uncond_nov}}, {{CLAIM:nov_null_t}}). With validators it cannot
be distinguished from it ({{CLAIM:anchor_uncond_gov}}, 95% CI {{CLAIM:gov_null_ci}}). No such comparison
exists for the conditional rate, whose denominator is written by the process under evaluation.

it entirely. Every irrigation cell carries two estimates of the governance effect on the increase
rate, the published conditional statistic (increases among decisions the agent itself labelled high
