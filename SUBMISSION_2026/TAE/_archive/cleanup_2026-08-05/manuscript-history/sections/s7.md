## 7 What to do instead <!-- page budget: 0.75 pg -->

The obvious repair does not work, and we tested it. Condition the norm on state the simulator writes
instead of on what the agent reports, and the antecedent moves as much or more: shortage tier 2 or
worse shifts {{CLAIM:world_tier2_shift}} against the self-report's
{{CLAIM:anchor_high_delta_bare}}, tier 1 or worse and a nonzero curtailment ratio each shift
{{CLAIM:world_tier1_curtail_shift}}, and a drought index at or above 0.5 shifts
{{CLAIM:world_drought_shift}}. In a coupled system the world is downstream of the treatment as well,
so moving the antecedent buys a differently observed conditioning variable, not a steadier one.

What survives is reporting discipline: print the conditioning variable's prevalence in both arms
beside the conformance rate, log the independent world state a self-reported antecedent refers to,
without which the number cannot be decomposed at any resolution, and give a design-fixed denominator
beside the conditional rate as a different estimand rather than a corrected effect size.
