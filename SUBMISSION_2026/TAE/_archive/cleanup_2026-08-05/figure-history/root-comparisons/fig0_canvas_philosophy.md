# Chromatic Ledger

The composition treats the diagram as a quiet editorial artifact rather than a software flowchart. Three large white forms establish a stable rhythm: an output, a rule, and a score. The shapes are deliberately broad and calm, with thin charcoal contours and generous margins. Meaning lives in the alignment between them; words are only anchors for what the geometry already makes visible.

One pale yellow field is the visual ledger. It begins inside the agent's output, passes beneath the rule, and becomes the denominator band in the score. No second accent competes with it. A single restrained red mark records interruption and return, while all ordinary structure remains black or grey. The color system is therefore semantic, not decorative: one field, one consequence, one exception.

Line-art pictograms keep the system human-readable at a glance: an output mark, an appraisal mark, a blocked rule, and a small score trace. They are simple enough to survive conference-scale reduction and specific enough to give each region a visual identity. Serif headers create a measured, publication-like voice; body labels remain plain and sparse. The drawing should feel like a carefully printed research plate, not an interface screenshot.

Balance comes from asymmetry held in check. The output and score carry the visual weight; the smaller rule box sits between them and interrupts the shared field without obscuring it. The red return mark supplies motion against an otherwise still composition. Every gap is intentional, every line has a job, and every label has breathing room. The final artifact should read as the product of painstaking craft: restrained, exact, and quietly memorable.
