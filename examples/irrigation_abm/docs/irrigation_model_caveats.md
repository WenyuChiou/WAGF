# Irrigation model caveats (2026-09-16 review)

The model is a stated simplification of Hung & Yang (2021) RL-ABM-CRSS, not a reproduction.

## Methods/SI Caveats

**Spec item 45m memory update (2026-10-08; supersedes caveat 14's count and
reflection-input sentence):** each decision prompt contains the five most
recent event memories plus up to two consolidated event memories with the
highest decayed importance, followed by the latest stored reflection in its
own final slot (at most 5 + 2 + 1). The reflection is excluded from both event
selections and therefore never consumes recent capacity. Event lines retain
the salience ordering of item 36l, including earlier-first importance ties.
The year-end reflection opts out of the separate slot, preserving its previous
outcome-only retrieval selection.

Limitation (spec items 43c and 45; literature scan `.research/design_specs/llm_agent_memory_reflection_lit_2026-10-07.md`, A4): "Reflections are written by the LLM and can misstate the agent's own history, as reported for generative agents (Park et al., 2023; Maharana et al., 2024). WAGF writes the factual years and amounts in the program. Mechanical checks block lessons with numbers when enabled, junk (no letters or fewer than three words), more than 500 characters, or foreign figures: one retry, then a drop. Semantic contradictions are report-only flags and never cause a retry or drop. Counts of stored, retried and dropped lessons and semantic flags by reason are reported per year in the manifest; every attempt is logged. The regex flags are measurements with known false positives, including negated claims, and are hand-audited in the pilot. Precision measured on the old prompt does not carry over to the new prompt. Checks that match words cannot catch every misstatement (in the cbb74f73 pilot, a hand sample found clear contradictions the checks missed in 5 of 30 unflagged reflections). Reflection is shown as a framework feature; we do not claim it makes behaviour more realistic, since published evidence for that rests on believability ratings (Larooij and Törnberg, 2025). Behavioural outcome metrics do not use reflection text; semantic-flag reports and integrity checks inspect it, and it enters the next year's decision prompt as memory."
